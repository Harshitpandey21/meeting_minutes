In the 2nd quarter 2023 earnings call, FinTech Plus reported a significant financial performance increase, showcasing a 25% rise in revenue to $125 million year-over-year and a 30% EBITDA margin amounting to $37.5 million. Net income rose from $10 million in Q2 2022 to $16 million, driven by expansions in high-yield savings products and a new RoboAdvisor platform. The company invested heavily in asset-backed securities and corporate bonds to enhance risk-adjusted returns. Total assets reached $1.5 billion, with an equity base of $600 million and a debt-to-equity ratio of 1.5. Customer acquisition costs dropped by 15% with lifetime value growing by 25%, reflecting organic user growth. FinTech Plus expects revenue of $135 million in the next quarter, bolstered by innovations in blockchain solutions and AI analytics. The upcoming IPO of subsidiary Pay Plus is expected to raise $200 million, supporting further growth strategies.
